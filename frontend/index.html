About this platform

Cross-Blockchain Passport Authentication is a secure identity verification platform that links Aadhaar identity to passport authentication without exposing personal data during the process.

What this platform does

  • Registers identity details on the Aadhaar blockchain.
  • Submits passport applications using a registered Aadhaar ID.
  • Cross-checks identity across two independent blockchain networks.
  • Records registrations, applications, and verification results immutably.

How privacy is protected

Name, date of birth, and Aadhaar number are converted into a secure cryptographic hash before blockchain storage. Digital signatures verify the proof, so systems exchange evidence of identity rather than raw identity data.

Why two blockchains

Aadhaar and Passport records remain on separate networks. No single system holds the complete identity picture; verification happens through secure cross-chain checks.
